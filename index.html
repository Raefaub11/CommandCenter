Non-Negotiable Protocol Rules
🌙
Tesa · Night Only Inject fasted, 2–3 hours post last meal. Aligns with natural nocturnal GH pulse. Magnesium nightly pairs with this for sleep amplification.
🚫
Never Stack Two GHRHs Tesa and CJC hit the same receptor. Running simultaneously = receptor competition, not amplification. Sequential only. Always.
💊
Magnesium · Nightly 30–60 min before bed only. Not morning. Maximizes sleep quality which directly amplifies your Tesa GH pulse efficiency.
🔄
CJC/IPA · 5 on / 2 off Weekly schedule: inject Mon–Fri, skip Sat–Sun. Mirrors natural weekend recovery and prevents receptor desensitization without a full month off. Take the weekends.
⚖️
Scale · Read the Floor Tesa water retention weeks 1–4 is real and temporary. Track your lowest reading, not individual spikes. The floor keeps dropping.
🥩
Protein · 120–150g Daily Non-negotiable for muscle retention during aggressive fat loss. The single most important nutrition lever in your entire protocol.
